# §AccAdv. قیدی

بسیاری از اسم‌های رایی مانند قید عمل کرده و معنای افعال را محدود می‌کنند. 

بیان نحوه‌ی انجام کار:  [τρόπον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CF%81%CF%8C%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BD) [τινά](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%B9%CE%BD%CE%AC) به نحوی، [τίνα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%AF%CE%BD%CE%B1) [τρόπον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CF%81%CF%8C%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BD) به چه نحوی؟، [τόνδε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CF%8C%CE%BD%CE%B4%CE%B5) ([τοῦτον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%BF%E1%BF%A6%CF%84%CE%BF%CE%BD)) [τὸν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B8%CE%BD) [τρόπον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CF%81%CF%8C%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BD) بدین نحو، [πάντα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%AC%CE%BD%CF%84%CE%B1) [τρόπον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CF%81%CF%8C%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BD) به هر نحوی، [τὴν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B4%CE%BD) [ταχίστην](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%B1%CF%87%CE%AF%CF%83%CF%84%CE%B7%CE%BD) به سریع‌ترین نحو، [τὴν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B4%CE%BD) [εὐθεῖαν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BD%90%CE%B8%CE%B5%E1%BF%96%CE%B1%CE%BD) مستقیماً.

→ “[**δίκην**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B4%CE%AF%CE%BA%CE%B7%CE%BD%2A%2A) [τοξότου](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%BF%CE%BE%CF%8C%CF%84%CE%BF%CF%85)” [**P. L. 705e**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plat.%20Laws%20705e&lang=original); ⇾ 

«به‌مانند کمانگیر»

→ “[ἔπλεε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%94%CF%80%CE%BB%CE%B5%CE%B5) [**πρόφασιν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%80%CF%81%CF%8C%CF%86%CE%B1%CF%83%CE%B9%CE%BD%2A%2A)/plee) [ἐπ᾽](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CF%80%E1%BE%BD) [Ἑλλησπόντου](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%99%CE%BB%CE%BB%CE%B7%CF%83%CF%80%CF%8C%CE%BD%CF%84%CE%BF%CF%85)p%27)” [**Hdt. 5.33**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Hdt.%205.33&lang=original); ⇾

«ظاهراً سوی هلسپونتس کشتی راند»

بیان درجه و مقدار:

بسیار، بسی [μέγα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AD%CE%B3%CE%B1), [μεγάλα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%B5%CE%B3%CE%AC%CE%BB%CE%B1)، فراوان [πολύ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BB%CF%8D), [πολλά](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BB%CE%BB%CE%AC)، اکثراً [τὸ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B8) [πολύ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BB%CF%8D), [τὰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B0) [πολλά](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BB%CE%BB%CE%AC)، تا حدی که، چنان [ὅσον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%85%CF%83%CE%BF%CE%BD) ، هیچ، هرگز [οὐδέν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90%CE%B4%CE%AD%CE%BD), [μηδέν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%B7%CE%B4%CE%AD%CE%BD)de/n)، آن سان، آنچنان [τοσοῦτον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%BF%CF%83%CE%BF%E1%BF%A6%CF%84%CE%BF%CE%BD)، قدری، برخی [τὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B6)، و [ἀρχήν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CF%81%CF%87%CE%AE%CE%BD) یا [τὴν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B4%CE%BD)rxh/n) [ἀρχήν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CF%81%CF%87%CE%AE%CE%BD) همراه [οὐ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90)rxh/n) یا [μή](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AE)) به معنی «از اساس»، «کلاً» یا «از ابتدا»:

→ “[ἐν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%BD) [τῷ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BF%B7)n) [παραχρῆμα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%B1%CF%81%CE%B1%CF%87%CF%81%E1%BF%86%CE%BC%CE%B1) [οὐκ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90%CE%BA) [ἔστιν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%94%CF%83%CF%84%CE%B9%CE%BD)k) [**ἀρχὴν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%80%CF%81%CF%87%E1%BD%B4%CE%BD%2A%2A)/stin) [ὀρθῶς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%80%CF%81%CE%B8%E1%BF%B6%CF%82)rxh\\n) [βουλεύεσθαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B2%CE%BF%CF%85%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%8D%CE%B5%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9)rqw=s)” [**Ant. 5.73**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Antiph.%205.73&lang=original); ⇾
