# §PerfNoni. Non-indicative[^79]

The tenses of the moods other than the indicative rarely have relative temporal value, however, they express the aspect of the verb. The aspect of a verb indicates the way an action extends over time and the way it is presented. For instance, It may present an action as a complete whole, or an incomplete, ongoing process. The perfect in non-indicative verbs presents the action as a state and emphasizes on the effect or results of an action that has already happened. 

With subjunctive:

→ “[ἵνα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%B5%CE%BD%CE%B1), [ἢν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A2%CE%BD) [μὴ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%E1%BD%B4)\\n) [ὑπακούωσι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%91%CF%80%CE%B1%CE%BA%CE%BF%CF%8D%CF%89%CF%83%CE%B9), [**τεθνήκωσιν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%84%CE%B5%CE%B8%CE%BD%CE%AE%CE%BA%CF%89%CF%83%CE%B9%CE%BD%2A%2A)” [**T. 8.74**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Thuc.%208.74&lang=original); ⇾ that, in case they do not submit, they may be put to death (lit. may be dead at once) 

With optative: In the optative, the reference is always to future time. The tenses do not refer to differences of time, and denote only the stage of the action.

→ “[**τεθναίης**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%84%CE%B5%CE%B8%CE%BD%CE%B1%CE%AF%CE%B7%CF%82%2A%2A) [ὦ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%A6) [Προῖτ᾽](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%A0%CF%81%CE%BF%E1%BF%96%CF%84%E1%BE%BD)=), [ἢ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A2) [κάκτανε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%AC%CE%BA%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%BD%CE%B5)\\) [Βελλεροφόντην](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%92%CE%B5%CE%BB%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%86%CF%8C%CE%BD%CF%84%CE%B7%CE%BD)” **[Hom. Il. 6.164](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0133%3Abook%3D6%3Acard%3D156)**; ⇾ either die thyself, Proetus, or slay Bellerophon

With Imperative, indicating completion with permanent result: 

→ “[**Τετάχθω**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%A4%CE%B5%CF%84%CE%AC%CF%87%CE%B8%CF%89%2A%2A)” [**P. R. 562a**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plat.%20Rep.%20562a&lang=original); ⇾ let him take his place (and stay there) 

→ “[**εἰρήσθω**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0%CF%81%CE%AE%CF%83%CE%B8%CF%89%2A%2A)” [**P. R. 503b**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plat.%20Rep.%20503b&lang=original); ⇾ let it have been said (once for all)

With infinitives, often of certainty of action:

→ “[οὐδὲ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90%CE%B4%E1%BD%B2)/n) [βουλεύεσθαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B2%CE%BF%CF%85%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%8D%CE%B5%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9)de\\) [ἔτι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%94%CF%84%CE%B9) [ὥρα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%A5%CF%81%CE%B1)/ti), [ἀλλὰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CE%BB%CE%BB%E1%BD%B0) [**βεβουλεῦσθαι**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B2%CE%B5%CE%B2%CE%BF%CF%85%CE%BB%CE%B5%E1%BF%A6%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9%2A%2A)lla\\)” [**P. Cr. 46a**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plat.%20Crito%2046a&lang=original); ⇾ it is time no longer to be making up one's mind, but to have it made up

With Participles:

→ “[καταλαμβάνουσι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%BB%CE%B1%CE%BC%CE%B2%CE%AC%CE%BD%CE%BF%CF%85%CF%83%CE%B9) [Βρασίδαν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%92%CF%81%CE%B1%CF%83%CE%AF%CE%B4%CE%B1%CE%BD) [**ἐπεληλυθότα**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%90%CF%80%CE%B5%CE%BB%CE%B7%CE%BB%CF%85%CE%B8%CF%8C%CF%84%CE%B1%2A%2A)” [**T. 3.69**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Thuc.%203.69&lang=original); ⇾ they found (historical present) that Brasidas had arrived

Ph. 778**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Soph.%20Phil.%20778&lang=original); ⇾ he who possessed it before
