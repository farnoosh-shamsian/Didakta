# §ImprComm. Command or Prohibition[^109]

Under commands are included requests, entreaties, summons, prescriptions, exhortations, etc. All imperative tenses refer to the future. The negative with the imperative verbs is [μή](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AE) and indicates prohibition. In exhortations [ἄγε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%84%CE%B3%CE%B5), [φέρε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%CE%AD%CF%81%CE%B5)/ge), [ἴθι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%B4%CE%B8%CE%B9) (usually with [δή](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%CE%AE)/qi), sometimes with [νύν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BD%CF%8D%CE%BD)), often precede the imperative.
