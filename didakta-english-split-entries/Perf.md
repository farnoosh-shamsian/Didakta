# §Perf. Completed action

The perfect denotes a completed action the effects of which still continue in the present: 

→ “[τὰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B0)pe/pleusan) [οἰκήματα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BC%B0%CE%BA%CE%AE%CE%BC%CE%B1%CF%84%CE%B1) [**ᾠκοδόμηται**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BE%A0%CE%BA%CE%BF%CE%B4%CF%8C%CE%BC%CE%B7%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%B9%2A%2A)kh/mata)” [**X. O. 9.2**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Ec.%209.2&lang=original); ⇾ the rooms have been constructed (their construction is finished) 

→ “[τὰς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B0%CF%82)|kodo/mhtai) [πόλεις](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CF%8C%CE%BB%CE%B5%CE%B9%CF%82) [αὐτῶν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD) [**παρῄρηται**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%80%CE%B1%CF%81%E1%BF%84%CF%81%CE%B7%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%B9%2A%2A)tw=n)” [**D. 9.26**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Dem.%209.26&lang=original); ⇾ he has taken away (and still holds) their cities 

→ “[τί](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%AF) [βουλεύεσθον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B2%CE%BF%CF%85%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%8D%CE%B5%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%BF%CE%BD) [ποιεῖν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%B9%CE%B5%E1%BF%96%CE%BD); [οὐδέν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90%CE%B4%CE%AD%CE%BD), [ἔφη](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%94%CF%86%CE%B7) [ὁ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%81)/fh) [Χαρμίδης](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%A7%CE%B1%CF%81%CE%BC%CE%AF%CE%B4%CE%B7%CF%82), [ἀλλὰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CE%BB%CE%BB%E1%BD%B0) [**βεβουλεύμεθα**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B2%CE%B5%CE%B2%CE%BF%CF%85%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%8D%CE%BC%CE%B5%CE%B8%CE%B1%2A%2A)lla\\)” [**P. Charm. 176c**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plat.%20Charm.%20176c&lang=original); ⇾ what are you conspiring to do? Nothing, said Charmides; we have already conspired

“[ἐφοβήθην](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CF%86%CE%BF%CE%B2%CE%AE%CE%B8%CE%B7%CE%BD), [καὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%E1%BD%B6)fobh/qhn) [ἔτι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%94%CF%84%CE%B9) [καὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%E1%BD%B6)/ti) [νῦν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BD%E1%BF%A6%CE%BD) [**τεθορύβημαι**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%84%CE%B5%CE%B8%CE%BF%CF%81%CF%8D%CE%B2%CE%B7%CE%BC%CE%B1%CE%B9%2A%2A)” [**Aes. 2.4**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Aeschin.%202.4&lang=original); ⇾ I was struck with fear, and even at the present moment am still in a state of agitation

When the perfect marks the enduring result rather than the completed act, it may not indicate any clear reference to the preceding action; for instance, [κέκλημαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%AD%CE%BA%CE%BB%CE%B7%CE%BC%CE%B1%CE%B9) (have received a name) am called, my name is, [κέκτημαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%AD%CE%BA%CF%84%CE%B7%CE%BC%CE%B1%CE%B9) (have acquired) possess, [μέμνημαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AD%CE%BC%CE%BD%CE%B7%CE%BC%CE%B1%CE%B9) (have recalled) remember, [τέθνηκα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%AD%CE%B8%CE%BD%CE%B7%CE%BA%CE%B1) (have passed away) am dead, [οἶδα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BC%B6%CE%B4%CE%B1) (have found out) know. These are often translated to a present tense and therefore, they are sometimes called “perfect with present meaning”; however, they are not fundamentally different from other perfect verbs.
