# §OptWish. Optative of Wish[^105]

The optative without [ἄν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%84%CE%BD)/n) is used to express a wish referring to the future.  (negative [μή](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AE)/n)). The optative of wish, or cupitive optative, is often introduced by [εἰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0) [γάρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B3%CE%AC%CF%81)), [εἴθε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B4%CE%B8%CE%B5) (Hom. [αἰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BC%B0)/qe) [γάρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B3%CE%AC%CF%81), [αἴθε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BC%B4%CE%B8%CE%B5)), or by [εἰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0)/qe), [ὡς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%A1%CF%82)) (both poetical).

→ “[ὦ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%A6) [παῖ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%B1%E1%BF%96)=), [**γένοιο**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B3%CE%AD%CE%BD%CE%BF%CE%B9%CE%BF%2A%2A) [πατρὸς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%B1%CF%84%CF%81%E1%BD%B8%CF%82) [εὐτυχέστερος](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BD%90%CF%84%CF%85%CF%87%CE%AD%CF%83%CF%84%CE%B5%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%82)” [**S. Aj. 550**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Soph.%20Aj.%20550&lang=original); ⇾ ah, boy, mayest thou prove more fortunate than thy sire

→ “[ὡς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%A1%CF%82) [**ὄλοιτο**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BD%84%CE%BB%CE%BF%CE%B9%CF%84%CE%BF%2A%2A)” [**S. El. 126**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Soph.%20El.%20126&lang=original); ⇾ may he perish

→ “[εἴθε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B4%CE%B8%CE%B5) [φίλος](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%CE%AF%CE%BB%CE%BF%CF%82)/qe) [ἡμῖν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A1%CE%BC%E1%BF%96%CE%BD) [**γένοιο**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B3%CE%AD%CE%BD%CE%BF%CE%B9%CE%BF%2A%2A)” [**X. H. 4.1.38**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Hell.%204.1.38&lang=original); ⇾ oh, if you would become our friend

The optative introduced by [εἰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0) [γάρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B3%CE%AC%CF%81)), etc. is sometimes explained as a protasis with the conclusion omitted: 

→ “[εἰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0) [γὰρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B3%E1%BD%B0%CF%81)) [**γένοιτο**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B3%CE%AD%CE%BD%CE%BF%CE%B9%CF%84%CE%BF%2A%2A)” [**X. C. 6.1.38**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Cyrop.%206.1.38&lang=original); ⇾ would that it might happen

An unattainable wish, referring to the present, may be expressed by the present optative in Homer:

→ “[εἴθ᾽](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B4%CE%B8%E1%BE%BD) [**ἡβώοιμι**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%A1%CE%B2%CF%8E%CE%BF%CE%B9%CE%BC%CE%B9%2A%2A)/q%27)” [**Hom. Il. 23.624**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0133%3Abook%3D23%3Acard%3D624); ⇾ would that I were young again

Under wishes are included execrations and protestations: 

→ “[**ἐξολοίμην**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%90%CE%BE%CE%BF%CE%BB%CE%BF%CE%AF%CE%BC%CE%B7%CE%BD%2A%2A)” [**Ar. Ach. 324**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Aristoph.%20Ach.%20324&lang=original); ⇾ may I perish

The optative may indicate rhetorical wishes, willingness, or indifferance as in imprecations.

→ “[ἐν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%BD) [πυρὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CF%85%CF%81%E1%BD%B6)n) [δὴ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%E1%BD%B4) [βουλαί](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B2%CE%BF%CF%85%CE%BB%CE%B1%CE%AF) [τε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%B5) [**γενοίατο**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B3%CE%B5%CE%BD%CE%BF%CE%AF%CE%B1%CF%84%CE%BF%2A%2A) [μήδεά](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AE%CE%B4%CE%B5%CE%AC) [τ᾽](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BE%BD) [ἀνδρῶν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CE%BD%CE%B4%CF%81%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD)” [**Hom. Il. 2.340**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0133%3Abook%3D2%3Acard%3D340); ⇾ into the fire let us cast all counsels and plans of warriors
