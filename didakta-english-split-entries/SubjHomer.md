# §SubjHomer. Anticipatory Subjunctive (Homeric)[^102]

In Homer the subjunctive is often closely akin to the future indicative, and refers by anticipation to a future event (negative [οὐ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90)).
