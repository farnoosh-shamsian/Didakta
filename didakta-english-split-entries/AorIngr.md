# §AorIngr. Ingressive Aorist[^67]

The aorist of verbs whose present denotes a state or a continued action, expresses the entrance into that state or the beginning of that action. The ingressive aorist is used with atelic verbs[^68], particularly with stative verbs and indicates the beginning of a state; for instance, [ἄρχω](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%84%CF%81%CF%87%CF%89) rule: [ἦρξα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A6%CF%81%CE%BE%CE%B1)/rxw) became ruler, [βασιλεύω](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B2%CE%B1%CF%83%CE%B9%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%8D%CF%89)=rca) am king, rule: [ἐβασίλευσα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%B2%CE%B1%CF%83%CE%AF%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%85%CF%83%CE%B1) became king, ascended the throne, or [νοσῶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BD%CE%BF%CF%83%E1%BF%B6)qa/rrhsa) am ill: [ἐνόσησα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%BD%CF%8C%CF%83%CE%B7%CF%83%CE%B1) fell ill.
