# §AorDram. Dramatic Aorist[^73]

The first person singular of the aorist is used in the answers and reactions in tragedy and comedy, often indicating a state of mind or an act expressing a state of mind (especially approval or disapproval) occurring to the speaker in the moment just passed. The  dramatic aorist is sometimes called instantaneous aorist.

→ “[**ἥσθην**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%A5%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B7%CE%BD%2A%2A), [ἐγέλασα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%B3%CE%AD%CE%BB%CE%B1%CF%83%CE%B1)” [**Ar. Eq. 696**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Aristoph.%20Kn.%20696&lang=original); ⇾ I am delighted, I can't help laughing

→ “[**ἐδεξάμην**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%90%CE%B4%CE%B5%CE%BE%CE%AC%CE%BC%CE%B7%CE%BD%2A%2A)ge/lasa) [τὸ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B8)deca/mhn) [ῥηθέν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BF%A5%CE%B7%CE%B8%CE%AD%CE%BD)” [**S. El. 668**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Soph.%20El.%20668&lang=original); ⇾ I welcome the omen 

Sometimes this use appears outside of dialogue:
