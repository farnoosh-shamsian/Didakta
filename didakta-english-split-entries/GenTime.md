# §GenTime. Time[^17]

The genitive denotes the time *within which*, or at a certain point *of which*, an action takes place. As contrasted with the accusative of time, the genitive signals a time frame for an action.

→ “[τὸν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B8%CE%BD) [μὲν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%E1%BD%B2%CE%BD) [χειμῶνα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%87%CE%B5%CE%B9%CE%BC%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD%CE%B1) [ὕει](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%95%CE%B5%CE%B9) [ὁ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%81) [θεός](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B8%CE%B5%CF%8C%CF%82), [τοῦ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%BF%E1%BF%A6) [δὲ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%E1%BD%B2) [**θέρεος**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B8%CE%AD%CF%81%CE%B5%CE%BF%CF%82%2A%2A) [χρηίσκονται](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%87%CF%81%CE%B7%CE%AF%CF%83%CE%BA%CE%BF%CE%BD%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%B9) [τῷ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BF%B7) [ὕδατι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%95%CE%B4%CE%B1%CF%84%CE%B9)” [**Hdt. 3.117**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Hdt.%203.117&lang=original); ⇾ during the (entire) *winter the god rains*, *but in* (a part of) summer they need the water

The genitive may denote the time *since* an action has happened or the time *until* an action will happen:

→ “[οὐδείς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90%CE%B4%CE%B5%CE%AF%CF%82) [μέ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AD)dei/s) [πω](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CF%89) [ἠρώτηκε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A0%CF%81%CF%8E%CF%84%CE%B7%CE%BA%CE%B5) [καινὸν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%CE%B9%CE%BD%E1%BD%B8%CE%BD)rw/thke) [οὐδὲν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90%CE%B4%E1%BD%B2%CE%BD) [**πολλῶν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BB%CE%BB%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD%2A%2A)de\\n) [**ἐτῶν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%90%CF%84%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD%2A%2A)” [**P. G. 448a**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plat.%20Gorg.%20448a&lang=original); ⇾ for many years nobody has put a new question to me

→ “[βασιλεὺς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B2%CE%B1%CF%83%CE%B9%CE%BB%CE%B5%E1%BD%BA%CF%82)tw=n) [οὐ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%90) [μαχεῖται](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%B1%CF%87%CE%B5%E1%BF%96%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%B9)) [**δέκα**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B4%CE%AD%CE%BA%CE%B1%2A%2A) [**ἡμερῶν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%A1%CE%BC%CE%B5%CF%81%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD%2A%2A)” [**X. A. 1.7.18**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Anab.%201.7.18&lang=original); ⇾ the king will not fight for ten days
