# §NomVoc. As Vocative[^4]

→ “[ὦ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%A6)di/pous) [**πόλις**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%80%CF%8C%CE%BB%CE%B9%CF%82%2A%2A)=) **[πόλις](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CF%8C%CE%BB%CE%B9%CF%82)**” **[Soph. OT 633](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Soph.+OT+633)**; ⇾ O city, city\!

→ “[**ὁ**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BD%81%2A%2A)se/rxomai) **[παῖς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%B1%E1%BF%96%CF%82)** [ἀκολούθει](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CE%BA%CE%BF%CE%BB%CE%BF%CF%8D%CE%B8%CE%B5%CE%B9) [δεῦρο](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%CE%B5%E1%BF%A6%CF%81%CE%BF)kolou/qei) [τὰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B0) [σκεύη](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%83%CE%BA%CE%B5%CF%8D%CE%B7) [φέρων](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%CE%AD%CF%81%CF%89%CE%BD)” [**Aristoph. Frogs 521**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Aristoph.+Frogs+521); ⇾ Follow, slave, and bring my baggage.
