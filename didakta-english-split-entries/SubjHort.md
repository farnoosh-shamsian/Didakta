# §SubjHort. Hortatory Subjunctive[^98]

The hortatory subjunctive (present or aorist) is used to express a request or a proposal (negative [μή](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AE)). The hortatory use of the subjunctive compensates for the absence of an imperative of the first person and is more common in the first person plural than first person singular.
