# §AutIden. Identicalness[^130]

After the article, in the attributive position, [αὐτός](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%CF%8C%CF%82)to/s) in any case means same. Thus, [ὁ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%81)to/s) [αὐτὸς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%E1%BD%B8%CF%82) [ἀνήρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CE%BD%CE%AE%CF%81)to\\s), rarely ([ὁ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%81)nh/r)) [ἀνὴρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CE%BD%E1%BD%B4%CF%81) [ὁ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%81)nh\\r) [αὐτός](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%CF%8C%CF%82) the same man. 

→ “[τοῦ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%BF%E1%BF%A6)to/s) [**αὐτοῦ**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%CE%BF%E1%BF%A6%2A%2A) [θέρους](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B8%CE%AD%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%85%CF%82)tou=)” [**T. 4.58**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Thuc.%204.58&lang=original); ⇾ in the same summer

→ “[τὰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B0) [**αὐτὰ**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%E1%BD%B0%2A%2A) [ταῦτα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%B1%E1%BF%A6%CF%84%CE%B1)ta\\)” [**X. A. 1.1.7**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Anab.%201.1.7&lang=original); ⇾ these same things

→ “[οἱ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BC%B1) [τοὺς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%BF%E1%BD%BA%CF%82) [**αὐτοὺς**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%CE%BF%E1%BD%BA%CF%82%2A%2A) [αἰεὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BC%B0%CE%B5%E1%BD%B6)tou\\s) [περὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%B5%CF%81%E1%BD%B6)ei\\) [τῶν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD) [**αὐτῶν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD%2A%2A) [λόγους](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BB%CF%8C%CE%B3%CE%BF%CF%85%CF%82)tw=n) [λέγοντες](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BB%CE%AD%CE%B3%CE%BF%CE%BD%CF%84%CE%B5%CF%82)” [**Ant. 5.50**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Antiph.%205.50&lang=original); ⇾ the people who are continually making the same speeches about the same things

→ “[ἐγὼ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%B3%E1%BD%BC) [μὲν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%E1%BD%B2%CE%BD)gw\\) [ὁ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%81) [**αὐτός**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%CF%8C%CF%82%2A%2A) [εἰμι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0%CE%BC%CE%B9)to/s), [ἱμεῖς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%B1%CE%BC%CE%B5%E1%BF%96%CF%82)mi) [δὲ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%E1%BD%B2) [μεταβάλλετε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%B5%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%B2%CE%AC%CE%BB%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%84%CE%B5)” [**T. 2.61**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Thuc.%202.61&lang=original); ⇾ I am the same, it is you who change

With a proper name or a word denoting an individual, the article is omitted
