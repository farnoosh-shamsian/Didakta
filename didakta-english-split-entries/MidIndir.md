# §MidIndir. Indirect Reflexive Middle[^86]

In the indirect reflexive middle, the subject of the verb is affected by the verb, often as a beneficiary. The Indirect Reflexive Middle represents the subject as acting *for himself, with reference to himself*, or *with something belonging to himself*. *Self* is often the indirect object. So [πορίζεσθαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BF%CF%81%CE%AF%CE%B6%CE%B5%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9)po/llusqai) provide for oneself ([πορίζειν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BF%CF%81%CE%AF%CE%B6%CE%B5%CE%B9%CE%BD) provide), [φυλάττεσθαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%CF%85%CE%BB%CE%AC%CF%84%CF%84%CE%B5%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9) guard against ([φυλάττειν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%CF%85%CE%BB%CE%AC%CF%84%CF%84%CE%B5%CE%B9%CE%BD) keep guard), [αἱρεῖσθαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BC%B1%CF%81%CE%B5%E1%BF%96%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9) choose (take for oneself). The middle often denotes that the subject acts with something belonging to himself (material objects, means, powers). It is often used for acts done willingly.
