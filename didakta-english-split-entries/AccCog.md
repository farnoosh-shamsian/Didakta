# §AccCog. Cognate[^38]

The cognate accusatives are objects added to specify the nature of the action of the verb and are either etymologically or semantically related to the verb. They can be nouns in the accusative that are of the *same origin* as the verb (they contain the same stem), or of *kindred meaning* with the verb (they express the same meaning). In many cases, the cognate accusative is left untranslated to avoid redundancy.
