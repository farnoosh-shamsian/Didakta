# §IndWish. Unattainable Wish[^96]

The past indicative with [εἴθε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B4%CE%B8%CE%B5) or with [εἰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0)/qe) [γάρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B3%CE%AC%CF%81)), without [ἄν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%84%CE%BD)/n), is used to express a present or past wish which cannot be realized. The imperfect refers to present time, the aorist to past time (negative [μή](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AE)).

→ “[εἴθ᾽](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B4%CE%B8%E1%BE%BD) [**εἶχες**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B5%E1%BC%B6%CF%87%CE%B5%CF%82%2A%2A)/q%27) [βελτἱους](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B2%CE%B5%CE%BB%CF%84%E1%BC%B1%CE%BF%CF%85%CF%82)=xes) [φρένας](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%CF%81%CE%AD%CE%BD%CE%B1%CF%82)” [**E. El. 1061**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Eur.%20El.%201061&lang=original); ⇾ would that thou hadst (now) a better heart

→ “[εἴθε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B4%CE%B8%CE%B5) [σοι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%83%CE%BF%CE%B9)/qe) [τότε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CF%8C%CF%84%CE%B5) [**συνεγενόμην**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%83%CF%85%CE%BD%CE%B5%CE%B3%CE%B5%CE%BD%CF%8C%CE%BC%CE%B7%CE%BD%2A%2A)” [**X. M. 1.2.46**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Mem.%201.2.46&lang=original); ⇾ would that I had then been with thee

An unattainable wish may also be expressed by [ὤφελον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%A4%CF%86%CE%B5%CE%BB%CE%BF%CE%BD) (ought) with the present or aorist infinitive: 

→ “[**ὤφελε**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BD%A4%CF%86%CE%B5%CE%BB%CE%B5%2A%2A)/felon) [Κῦρος](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%9A%E1%BF%A6%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%82)/fele) [**ζῆν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B6%E1%BF%86%CE%BD%2A%2A)” [**X. A. 2.1.4**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Anab.%202.1.4&lang=original); ⇾ would that Cyrus were (now) alive (Cyrus ought to be alive) 

→ “[εἰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0)/felon) [γὰρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B3%E1%BD%B0%CF%81)) [**ὤφελον**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BD%A4%CF%86%CE%B5%CE%BB%CE%BF%CE%BD%2A%2A) [οἷοί](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BC%B7%CE%BF%CE%AF)/felon) [τε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%B5) [**εἶναι**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B5%E1%BC%B6%CE%BD%CE%B1%CE%B9%2A%2A) [οἱ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BC%B1)=nai) [πολλοὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BB%CE%BB%CE%BF%E1%BD%B6) [κακὰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%CE%BA%E1%BD%B0) [ἐργάζεσθαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CF%81%CE%B3%CE%AC%CE%B6%CE%B5%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9)” **[Pl. Cr. 44d](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plat.%20Crito%2044d&lang=original)**; ⇾ would that the multitude were able to do evil
