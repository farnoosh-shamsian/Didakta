# §MidDir. Direct Reflexive Middle[^87]

The Direct Reflexive Middle represents the subject as acting directly *on themselves*. *Self* is here the direct object. Direct reflexive middle verbs often express external and natural acts, such as grooming: [ἀλείφεσθαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CE%BB%CE%B5%CE%AF%CF%86%CE%B5%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9)ki/a_n) anoint oneself or [λοῦσθαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BB%CE%BF%E1%BF%A6%CF%83%CE%B8%CE%B1%CE%B9)lei/fesqai) wash oneself. The part affected may be added in the accusative.

Plut. 85](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Aristoph.%20Pl.%2085&lang=original)**; ⇾ He has not bathed since his birth
