# §PresIter. Customary Action (Iterative)[^50]

The present is used to express a customary or repeated action: 

→ “[οὗτος](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BD%97%CF%84%CE%BF%CF%82) [μὲν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%E1%BD%B2%CE%BD) [γὰρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B3%E1%BD%B0%CF%81) [ὕδωρ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BD%95%CE%B4%CF%89%CF%81), [ἐγὼ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%B3%E1%BD%BC) [δ᾽](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%E1%BE%BD)gw\\) [οἶνον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BF%E1%BC%B6%CE%BD%CE%BF%CE%BD) [**πίνω**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CF%80%CE%AF%CE%BD%CF%89%2A%2A)=non)” [**D. 19.46**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Dem.%2019.46&lang=original); ⇾ for this man drinks water, whereas I drink wine

The present indicative may also indicate a general truth and could be used to express an action that is true for all time. The present is an *absolute* tense in such sentences. The future, aorist, and perfect may also express a general truth.

→ “[τὰ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B0)) [δὶς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%E1%BD%B6%CF%82) [πέντε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%AD%CE%BD%CF%84%CE%B5) [δέκα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%CE%AD%CE%BA%CE%B1) [**ἐστίν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%90%CF%83%CF%84%CE%AF%CE%BD%2A%2A)” [**Xen. Mem. 4.4.7**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.+Mem.+4.4.7&fromdoc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0208); ⇾ two times five is ten

→ “[**ἄγει**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%84%CE%B3%CE%B5%CE%B9%2A%2A) [δὲ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%E1%BD%B2)/gei) [πρὸς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CF%81%E1%BD%B8%CF%82) [φῶς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%E1%BF%B6%CF%82) [τὴν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B4%CE%BD) [ἀλήθειαν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CE%BB%CE%AE%CE%B8%CE%B5%CE%B9%CE%B1%CE%BD) [χρόνος](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%87%CF%81%CF%8C%CE%BD%CE%BF%CF%82)lh/qeian)” **Men. Sent. 11**; ⇾ time brings the truth to light
