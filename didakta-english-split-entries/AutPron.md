# §AutPron. Personal Pronoun[^129]

When [αὐτός](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%CF%8C%CF%82) is unemphatic and standing alone in the cases other than nominative, it has the meaning of the personal pronoun of the third person (him, her, it, them). This use of [αὐτός](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%CF%8C%CF%82) as a personal pronoun always refers to someone or something that is already introduced. 

→ “[ἐκέλευον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%BA%CE%AD%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%85%CE%BF%CE%BD)to/s) [**αὐτὴν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%E1%BD%B4%CE%BD%2A%2A)ke/leuon) [ἀπιέναι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CF%80%CE%B9%CE%AD%CE%BD%CE%B1%CE%B9)th\\n)” [**L. 1.12**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Lys.%201.12&lang=original); ⇾ they ordered her to depart

→ “[ἄρχει](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%84%CF%81%CF%87%CE%B5%CE%B9)di/pous) [τις](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%B9%CF%82)/rxei) [**αὐτῶν**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%E1%BF%B6%CE%BD%2A%2A) [ἢ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A2)tw=n) '[πὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%E1%BD%B6)\\) [τῷ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BF%B7) [πλήθει](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%BB%CE%AE%CE%B8%CE%B5%CE%B9) [λόγος](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BB%CF%8C%CE%B3%CE%BF%CF%82);” [**Soph. OC 33**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0189%3Acard%3D33); ⇾ Does someone govern them, or does authority rest with the masses?
