# §IndOblig. Unfulfilled Obligation[^95]

With impersonal verbs indicating obligation, propriety, necessity, or possibility, the indicative without [ἄν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%84%CE%BD)/n) is used to express an unfulfilled obligation.  Such expressions are [ἔδει](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%94%CE%B4%CE%B5%CE%B9)pwllu/meqa), [χρῆν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%87%CF%81%E1%BF%86%CE%BD)/dei) (or [ἐχρῆν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CF%87%CF%81%E1%BF%86%CE%BD)), [προσῆκε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%83%E1%BF%86%CE%BA%CE%B5)xrh=n), [καιρὸς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%CE%B9%CF%81%E1%BD%B8%CF%82) [ἦν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A6%CE%BD), [ἄξιον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%84%CE%BE%CE%B9%CE%BF%CE%BD)=n) [ἦν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A6%CE%BD)/cion), [εἰκὸς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B0%CE%BA%E1%BD%B8%CF%82)=n) [ἦν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A6%CE%BD)ko\\s), [δίκαιον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%CE%AF%CE%BA%CE%B1%CE%B9%CE%BF%CE%BD)=n) [ἦν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A6%CE%BD), [αἰσχρὸν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BC%B0%CF%83%CF%87%CF%81%E1%BD%B8%CE%BD)=n) [ἦν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A6%CE%BD)sxro\\n), [ἐξῆν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%90%CE%BE%E1%BF%86%CE%BD)=n), [καλῶς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%CE%BB%E1%BF%B6%CF%82)ch=n) [εἶχεν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B5%E1%BC%B6%CF%87%CE%B5%CE%BD), verbals in \-[τόν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CF%8C%CE%BD)=xen) or \-[τέον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%AD%CE%BF%CE%BD) with [ἦν](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A6%CE%BD), etc. Here, the indicative denotes that an action contrary to what has happened (often expressed by an infinitive) was or is required. This use of the indicative is a variation of the counterfactual indicative.
