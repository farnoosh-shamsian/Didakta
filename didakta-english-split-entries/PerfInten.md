# §PerfInten. Intensive Perfect[^76]

The perfects tense with atelic verbs[^77] may indicate an emphasis of the degree of the state expressed by the verb. These are often called intensive perfects. Such are: verbs of the *senses* ([δέδορκα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B4%CE%AD%CE%B4%CE%BF%CF%81%CE%BA%CE%B1)=da) gaze, [πέφρικα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%AD%CF%86%CF%81%CE%B9%CE%BA%CE%B1) shudder), of *sustained sound* ([κέκραγα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%AD%CE%BA%CF%81%CE%B1%CE%B3%CE%B1) bawl, [λέληκα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BB%CE%AD%CE%BB%CE%B7%CE%BA%CE%B1) shout, [βέβρυχα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B2%CE%AD%CE%B2%CF%81%CF%85%CF%87%CE%B1) roar), of *emotion* ([πεφόβημαι](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%80%CE%B5%CF%86%CF%8C%CE%B2%CE%B7%CE%BC%CE%B1%CE%B9) am filled with alarm, [γέγηθα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B3%CE%AD%CE%B3%CE%B7%CE%B8%CE%B1) am glad, [μέμηλε](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%AD%CE%BC%CE%B7%CE%BB%CE%B5) cares for), of *gesture* ([κέχηνα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%AD%CF%87%CE%B7%CE%BD%CE%B1) keep the mouth agape), and many others ([σεσίγηκα](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%83%CE%B5%CF%83%CE%AF%CE%B3%CE%B7%CE%BA%CE%B1) am still, etc.).
