# §ArtSubs. As Substantivizer[^122]

When the article is added to a word or word group other than a noun, it is substantivised. The article has the power to make a noun of any word or words to which it is prefixed. It can be used with adjectives, participles, prepositional phrases, adverbs, attributive genitives, and whole word groups, sentences and clauses.
