# §ArtAbst. چەمکی لێ‌دابڕاو(ئابستراکت)

وشە و چەمکی لێ‌دابڕاو زۆرجار لەگەڵ پیتی پێناسە‌ بەکاردێت.  
→ “[**ἡ**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%A1%2A%2A) [ἀρετὴ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%80%CF%81%CE%B5%CF%84%E1%BD%B4) [μᾶλλον](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%E1%BE%B6%CE%BB%CE%BB%CE%BF%CE%BD)reth%5C) [ἢ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BC%A2) [**ἡ**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%A1%2A%2A)%5C) [φυγὴ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%CF%85%CE%B3%E1%BD%B4) [σῴζει](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%83%E1%BF%B4%CE%B6%CE%B5%CE%B9) [τὰς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%E1%BD%B0%CF%82) [ψυχάς](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%88%CF%85%CF%87%CE%AC%CF%82)” [**X. C. 4.1.5**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Cyrop.%204.1.5&lang=original); ⇾   
«ئازایەتی زۆرتر له هەڵاتن ژیان پاشەکەوت دەکات»  
→ “[**ἡ**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%A1%2A%2A) [σωφροσύνη](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%83%CF%89%CF%86%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%83%CF%8D%CE%BD%CE%B7) … [καὶ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%E1%BD%B6)ndrei/a) [αὐτὴ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%B1%E1%BD%90%CF%84%E1%BD%B4) [**ἡ**](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%2A%2A%E1%BC%A1%2A%2A)th%5C) [φρόνησις](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%86%CF%81%CF%8C%CE%BD%CE%B7%CF%83%CE%B9%CF%82) [μὴ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%E1%BD%B4) [καθαρμός](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BA%CE%B1%CE%B8%CE%B1%CF%81%CE%BC%CF%8C%CF%82) [τις](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%B9%CF%82) [ᾖ](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%E1%BE%96)” [**Plat. Phaedo 69c**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0169%3Atext%3DPhaedo%3Asection%3D69c); ⇾  
« خۆپارێزی و حیکمەت ڕەنگە جۆرێک لە پاکژکردنەوە بێت»  
بەڵام زۆرجار ناوی چاکی و خراپەکاری و هونەر و زانست و پیشەکان پیتی پێناسە ناگرن.  
→ “[τί](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%AF) [σωφροσύνη](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%83%CF%89%CF%86%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%83%CF%8D%CE%BD%CE%B7), [τί](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CF%84%CE%AF) [μανία](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/%CE%BC%CE%B1%CE%BD%CE%AF%CE%B1);” [**X. M. 1.1.16**](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.%20Mem.%201.1.16&lang=original); ⇾   
«خۆڕاگری چییە؟، شێتی چییە؟»
